Bespoke Swag Craftsmanship & Engineering Excellence

Every component inside Custom Swag Factory kits is manufactured under rigorous ISO 9001:2015 engineering controls in Jinjiang, Fujian. Unlike trading companies that consolidate mismatched off-the-shelf items, we control the tooling, laser engraving, micro-injection dispensing, and custom foam die-cutting in our own audited facility.

  • Zero Toxic Formulations: All polymers are tested 100% free of phthalates, BPA, and heavy metals (<10 ppm Pb), compliant with US CPSIA and California Prop 65.
  • Precision Laser Etching: High-speed 50W fiber laser engraving machines achieve razor-sharp vector logos with 0.05mm edge precision across powder-coated drinkware and zinc alloy metals.
  • FSC-Certified & RPET Materials: Hardcover notebooks utilize 100% post-consumer recycled RPET fabric with 80gsm FSC-certified acid-free archival inner paper.

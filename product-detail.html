Micro-Injection & Precision Metal Craftsmanship

Every promotional craft manufactured by NAIKE GIFTS (Jinjiang Naike Promo Co., Ltd.) leverages Jinjiang's world-class polymer molding and precision die-casting ecosystem. From multi-cavity CNC mold engraving to automated micro-injection dispensing and electroplating, we maintain the highest international quality benchmarks.

  • Precision Multi-Color Dispensing: 12-color automatic micro-injection dispensing heads guaranteeing zero bleeding and ultra-sharp 0.3mm relief line definition.
  • Eco-Grade Polymer & Metal Alloys: High-purity #3 zinc alloy, food-grade liquid silicone, and phthalate-free soft PVC compliant with EU REACH & California Prop 65.
  • Superior Hardware & Fasteners: Reinforced calibrated POM buttons, butterfly clutches, safety pin clasps, and heavy-duty swivel keyrings tested to rigorous tensile thresholds.

📊 Laboratory Test Thresholds

• Rivet / Button Pull-Off Force: >25 lbs (110 N) tested to ASTM F963 / EN71-1

• Salt Spray Corrosion Resistance: 48h–96h neutral salt spray test (ASTM B117)

• Cross-Cut Paint Adhesion: Class 0 / 5B zero flaking (ISO 2409 / ASTM D3359)

• Chemical Safety Screen: Lead <90ppm, Cadmium <100ppm, Phthalates <1000ppm

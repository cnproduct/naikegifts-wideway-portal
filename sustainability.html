Four Environmental Pillars of Our Manufacturing Facility

♻️
Pillar 1: Circular Polymers

Global Recycled Standard (GRS 4.0) RPET

Our bespoke corporate mascot plush toys, branded lanyard neck straps, tote bags, and presentation pouch linings are constructed from 100% post-consumer recycled polyethylene terephthalate (RPET) sourced from recycled beverage bottles. Every batch is certified under Control Union GRS 4.0 Scope Certificate (CU1094821), with complete chain-of-custody Transaction Certificates (TC) provided for your enterprise sustainability audits.

  • Ocean Bound Plastic Divergence: A single 20cm custom plush toy diverts on average 8 post-consumer plastic bottles from marine ecosystems.
  • Reduced Carbon Intensity: GRS RPET fiber reduces greenhouse gas emissions by up to 52% compared to virgin polyester yarn.
  • Sensory Excellence: Advanced fine-denier spinning ensures that recycled crystal velvet retains identical ultra-soft hand feel and tactile luxury.
🪙
Pillar 2: Metallurgy Stewardship

100% Recyclable Zinc Alloy Metallurgy

In our metal casting division, 100% of medals, challenge coins, lapel pin bases, and keychains utilize premium Zamak-3 zinc alloy. Unlike traditional brass or bronze foundry work, Zamak-3 features a low melting point of 385°C–395°C, reducing thermal energy requirements by over 60% and enabling instantaneous on-site remelting of sprues and runners.

  • Zero Raw Scrap Waste: Over 99.2% of runner scrap is collected, re-ingoted, and cycled back into centrifugal casting crucibles without metallurgical degradation.
  • Non-Hazardous Foundry Byproducts: Zinc dross is collected by licensed industrial recyclers for secondary chemical refining, leaving zero hazardous slag in local waste streams.
  • Permanent Durability: Finished medals withstand 72-hour ASTM B117 neutral salt spray corrosion testing without structural decay, creating lifetime commemorative value.
💧
Pillar 3: Water & Air Filtration

Closed-Loop Water Recovery & VOC Scrubbing

Our industrial campus in Jinjiang operates in strict compliance with Chinese Grade-A National Environmental Standards and ISO 14001:2015. Surface preparation, ultrasonic mold washing, and pre-plating rinsing utilize an advanced three-stage reverse osmosis (RO) closed-loop filtration system.

  • 98% Recirculation Rate: Process rinse water is continually deionized and returned to cleaning tanks, reducing freshwater withdrawal to minimal makeup levels.
  • Heavy Metal Ion Trapping: Ion-exchange membrane filters isolate copper and nickel traces, preventing municipal waterway contamination.
  • Activated Carbon VOC Scrubbing: High-temperature vulcanization ovens and dispensing stations vent through catalytic activated carbon adsorption towers, keeping exhaust VOC emissions < 20 mg/m³.
🌿
Pillar 4: Green Polymer Chemistry

Phthalate-Free & Bio-Based Formulations

All soft PVC shoe charms, bar service mats, rubber keychains, and 3D silicone merchandise are formulated with high-molecular-weight non-phthalate plasticizers (DOTP and DINCH) paired with virgin eco-grade polymer resin.

  • Total Lead Content < 10 ppm: Vastly outperforming the US CPSIA 90 ppm regulatory ceiling, verified by SGS XRF and acid digestion testing.
  • California Proposition 65 Compliant: Zero listed reproductive or developmental toxins, completely eliminating mandatory warning label requirements in North America.
  • Food-Contact Silicone Alternatives: Drinkware coasters and lunchware swag available in 100% platinum-cured food-grade silicone compliant with FDA 21 CFR 177.2600 and German LFGB.

Frequently Asked Questions: B2B ESG & Sustainability Procurement

1. How does Custom Swag Factory verify that RPET materials are genuinely post-consumer recycled?

Every shipment of our recycled plush toys, lanyards, and packaging is verified through Global Recycled Standard (GRS 4.0) Transaction Certificates (TC) issued by independent certifying bodies such as Control Union. These certificates trace the raw polymer feedstock directly to post-consumer collection facilities and verify the exact percentage of recycled content throughout spinning, weaving, and sewing.

2. Can you provide third-party documentation for Scope 3 emissions reporting?

Yes. We provide comprehensive unredacted carbon footprint breakdowns, energy consumption logs, and material mass balance records verified under our ISO 14001:2015 Environmental Management System and SEDEX 4-Pillar SMETA audits. Compliance directors can directly link our profile on the Sedex Advance platform (ID: ZC41920847) to import auditable supplier ESG metrics into your corporate sustainability dashboards.

3. Are your custom shoe charms and soft PVC merchandise compliant with EU REACH SVHC and California Prop 65?

Absolutely. Every batch of our liquid polymer compound is tested by accredited laboratories (SGS, Intertek, TÜV Rheinland) against 240+ Substances of Very High Concern (SVHC) under EU REACH regulations, as well as California Proposition 65 standards for lead (<10 ppm) and ortho-phthalates (non-detectable < 50 ppm). Full chemical lab dossiers are available upon request.

4. What sustainable packaging alternatives do you offer for corporate swag kits?

We offer 100% plastic-free packaging options including FSC-certified unbleached corrugated kraft boxes, certified compostable cornstarch PLA polybags, organic cotton drawstring bags, and recycled RPET felt pouches. Printing is executed exclusively with non-toxic, soy-based inks that are fully biodegradable and curbside recyclable.

5. How do you protect client molds and intellectual property during recycling processes?

Under Pillar 4 of our valid SEDEX 4-Pillar audit (Business Ethics), our CNC mold production, storage, and maintenance facilities operate under strict biometric access control and 24/7 CCTV surveillance. Defective castings, off-spec test units, and runner trimmings are granulated or remelted within locked custody zones to prevent any trademarked or proprietary client designs from leaving our secure campus.

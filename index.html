NAIKE GIFTS Enterprise Profile • Verified Jinjiang Promotional Manufacturer Summary

Jinjiang Naike Promo Co., Ltd. (Naike Gifts / 晋江耐客生态科技有限公司) operates a specialized 1,650㎡ clean manufacturing campus in Chendai, Jinjiang, Fujian—China's footwear and light industrial hub. Integrating high-speed automated micro-injection dispensing machines, CNC engraving centers, high-tonnage zinc die casting, and Tajima digital embroidery, Naike Gifts delivers 5,000,000+ units monthly. All products are backed by SEDEX SMETA 4-Pillar, amfori BSCI, ISO 9001:2015, US CPSIA Zero-Lead, California Prop 65, and EN71-1/2/3 compliance. We provide 12-hour free 3D CAD mockups, 3–5 day physical sample turnaround, and direct DDP shipping worldwide.

Global Client Case Studies

Real-world delivery records for world marathon organizers, viral direct-to-consumer footwear accessories, and Silicon Valley corporate swag programs.

D2C VIRAL ACCESSORY

500,000 Pcs Glow-in-the-Dark Sneaker Shoe Charms

A leading US TikTok accessory brand required an expedited 500,000 pcs drop of 3D glow-in-the-dark miniature sneaker charms for foam clogs. NAIKE GIFTS mobilized 4 multi-cavity CNC molds and 6 auto-dispensing lines.

Results: Completed in 14 calendar days. Zero button detachments reported across 500k units (100% passed 25 lbs pull test).
WORLD MARATHON SERIES

45,000 Pcs 360° Kinetic Spinning Finisher Medals

A premier European marathon required high-relief zinc alloy finisher medals featuring an independently spinning central architectural spinner, translucent stained-glass enamel, and heat-transfer sublimated neck ribbons.

Results: Delivered DDP to Frankfurt 10 days ahead of race day. Rated 4.9/5 in runner feedback surveys.
ENTERPRISE TECH SWAG

10,000 Sets Curated Mascot Plush & Pin Welcome Boxes

A Silicon Valley fintech unicorn commissioned custom welcome kits for global employees: a 20cm GRS-certified recycled mascot plush toy, hard enamel corporate anniversary pin, and zinc alloy flight keytag.

Results: Full turnkey assembly in custom magnetic gift boxes with individual fulfillment to North America, Europe, and Asia hubs.
GLOBAL BEVERAGE BRAND

30,000 Pcs Heavy-Duty 10mm Spill-Proof Bar Service Mats

A multinational spirits conglomerate needed heavy-gauge (60×10×1cm) PVC bar service spill mats with high-definition molded 3D brand logos and anti-slip studded surface for pub chains across the UK and Australia.

Results: Passed 100% FDA food contact migration testing with zero odor and dishwasher-safe longevity.

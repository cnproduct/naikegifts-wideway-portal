Toy & Consumer Product Directives

Chemical & Physical Safety Compliance

🇺🇸 US CPSIA & California Prop 65

Total lead content < 90 ppm, cadmium < 100 ppm, and non-detectable phthalates (DEHP, DBP, BBP, DINP, DIDP, DnOP < 1,000 ppm). Passed under CPSC accredited laboratories.

🇪🇺 European EN71-1/2/3 Directives

Mandatory compliance for promotional toys and shoe accessories: EN71-1 (Mechanical & Physical Properties), EN71-2 (Flammability), and EN71-3 (Migration of 19 Heavy Elements).

For custom silicone bar mats, drink coasters, and silicone straw toppers. Certified BPA-free, odorless, platinum-cured silicone compliant with US FDA 21 CFR 177.2600 & German LFGB.

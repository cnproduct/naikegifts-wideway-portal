Inside Our Valid SEDEX 4-Pillar Audit (SMETA 6.1 / 7.0)

Unlike basic 2-Pillar factories that only check labor and safety, CUSTOM SWAG FACTORY maintains a full Valid SEDEX 4-Pillar Audit. Multinational corporations, UK/EU retail chains, and Fortune 500 procurement teams require all 4 pillars to satisfy modern ESG, CSDDD, and ethical supply chain mandates:

PILLAR 01

Labor Standards & ETI Base Code

100% conformance with the Ethical Trading Initiative (ETI) Base Code and ILO conventions: freely chosen employment, zero child or bonded labor, verified biometric timecards, statutory overtime wage premiums (150%/200%/300%), social insurance coverage, non-discrimination, and democratic worker representation committees.

PILLAR 02

PILLAR 03 • 4-PILLAR EXCLUSIVE

Environmental Assessment & Stewardship

Comprehensive environmental audit verifying ISO 14001 EMS permits, activated-carbon exhaust scrubbing, closed-loop cooling water recirculation (92% water reuse), hazardous waste licensed disposal, zero-lead & non-phthalate raw material lifecycle controls, GRS 4.0 recycled RPET integration, and Scope 1/2 carbon tracking.

PILLAR 04 • 4-PILLAR EXCLUSIVE

Audited corporate governance enforcing zero-tolerance anti-bribery and anti-corruption policies, anonymous whistleblower channels, transparent customs and financial bookkeeping, strict client NDA enforcement, restricted-access CNC mold storage vaults for licensed IP, and ethical sub-tier material supplier vetting.

Quality Inspection Pipeline

Our 5-Gate Quality Assurance Architecture

Independent QA officers monitor raw material verification, mold micro-dispensing, electroplating, and final packaging.

GATE 1

IQC: Incoming Raw Materials

Every batch of soft PVC pellets, liquid silicone, #3 zinc alloy ingots, and RPET plush fabrics undergoes strict incoming testing:

  • XRF heavy metal screening (Lead <90ppm, Cadmium <100ppm)
  • Zero phthalates certification (DINP, DIDP, DEHP, DBP zero-detect)
  • Zinc alloy purity verification (#3 high-density formulation)
  • Fabric tensile strength & AZO-free dye certification
GATE 2

IPQC: Mold & Color Matching

High-speed automated micro-injection dispensing inspected continuously on the shopfloor:

  • Pantone PMS Solid Coated match verified under D65 standard light
  • Zero color bleeding between adjacent micro-relief chambers
  • Laser micrometer check for thickness uniformity (±0.05 mm)
  • High-temp vulcanization curing curve (180°C–220°C monitored)
GATE 3

IPQC: Mechanical Pull Testing

Destructive tensile and pull tests conducted on representative samples from every 500 pcs:

  • Shoe charm POM rivet pull-force > 25 lbs (110 N) tested
  • Lapel pin spur nail & butterfly clutch shear resistance test
  • Medal jump ring & ribbon stitched loop tensile load > 20 kg
  • Keychain swivel ring 5,000-cycle rotational endurance check
GATE 4

IPQC: Corrosion & Adhesion

Electroplated medals, coins, and enamel surfaces tested under rigorous environmental chambers:

  • ASTM B117 48h to 96h neutral salt spray with zero tarnish or pitting
  • Cross-cut tape adhesion test (ISO 2409 Class 0 / 5B zero flaking)
  • UV weathering chamber solar exposure (no yellowing or fading)
  • Abrasion and scratch resistance on antique brushed finishes
GATE 5

FQC: 100% Needle Detector & Audit

Final screening before master carton sealing and export palletization:

  • 100% plush toys passed through 0.8mm conveyor needle detector
  • AQL 1.0 (Critical) / 2.5 (Major) pre-shipment sampling audit
  • Barcode scanner verification on retail blister packaging
  • ISTA 1A master carton drop test (10 drops from 76 cm height)

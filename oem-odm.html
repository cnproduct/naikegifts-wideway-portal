Our 6-Stage End-to-End Customization Workflow

Engineered for world-class quality, rapid turnaround, and zero defect tolerance.

STEP 01
📐

Free 3D CAD & Vector Artwork (12h)

Submit your 2D AI, PDF, or high-res sketch. Our 3D engineering team generates photo-realistic 3D CAD renders with Pantone PMS color matching and layer height callouts within 12 hours.

STEP 02
⚙️

High-Speed CNC Tooling (5–7d)

Our in-house 5-axis CNC machining centers engrave steel, aluminum, and copper electrode molds with ±0.02mm precision, capturing micro-relief textures, 3D curved surfaces, and recessed enamel chambers.

STEP 03
🧪

Automated Micro-Injection & Die Casting

12 multi-color automatic dispensing heads inject phthalate-free soft PVC or liquid silicone with zero bleeding. High-pressure zinc alloy die-casting stations produce clean, burr-free metal medals and coins.

STEP 04
🎨

Hard Enamel, Infill & Electroplating

Hand-infilled synthetic cloisonné hard enamel, vibrant soft enamel, glitter, glow-in-the-dark, and translucent finishes. Advanced plating options: 24K real gold, antique bronze, matte black, and iridescent rainbow anodizing.

STEP 05
🔬

100% Quality & Safety Testing

Every batch undergoes 25 lbs (110 N) button pull-force testing, 48–96h salt spray corrosion resistance, cross-cut enamel adhesion verification (Class 0), and 0.8mm metal needle scanning for mascot plush toys.

STEP 06
📦

Custom Packaging & Global DDP Logistics

Custom branded backing cards, retail blister boxes, velvet cases, and turnkey curated corporate swag mailer boxes. Delivered seamlessly with all import customs duties and taxes prepaid (DDP).

Frequently Asked OEM/ODM Questions

What format should I provide my artwork or logo in?

We prefer vector files in AI, PDF, EPS, or SVG format with all fonts converted to outlines. If you only have high-resolution PNG/JPG files or hand sketches, our design team can vectorize and convert them into 3D CAD mockups for free within 12 hours.

Can you match our exact corporate Pantone PMS brand colors?

Yes. We calibrate liquid PVC resins, silicone pigments, and enamel enamels to the Pantone Matching System (PMS Solid Coated) with spectrophotometer tolerance under Delta E < 1.0, ensuring 100% brand consistency across all promotional items.

Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA) before receiving our designs?

Absolutely. We strictly enforce mutual NDAs with all clients. Your proprietary character designs, corporate logos, and custom mold tooling remain 100% exclusive and are never shared, displayed, or re-sold to third parties.

What are the payment terms for custom manufacturing?

Our standard terms are 30% deposit upon pre-production sample sign-off, and 70% balance prior to shipment or against the Bill of Lading (B/L). For established long-term partners and enterprise clients, flexible OA 30/60 days credit terms can be approved.

From Tooling Roots to Global Export Leader

Engineering Integrity Built into Every Single Piece

Established in 2008 in Chendai town, Jinjiang—the core epicenter of China's polymer injection, mold fabrication, and footwear manufacturing—Jinjiang Naike Promo Co., Ltd. (NAIKE GIFTS) has spent nearly two decades mastering high-precision micro-injection dispensing and metal die casting.

Unlike conventional trading intermediaries, Naike Gifts owns and operates its self-built 1,650 m² modern manufacturing plant equipped with automated 12-color liquid PVC dispensers, 5-axis high-speed CNC mold carving machines, high-pressure zinc alloy die-casting stations, automated Grosgrain ribbon sublimation printers, and Tajima digital embroidery lines.

Zero Non-Conformances: Fully audited and certified under SEDEX SMETA 4-Pillar (Company ID: ZC41920847), amfori BSCI (Grade A), and ISO 9001:2015. All materials comply with US CPSIA zero-lead (<10 ppm), California Prop 65, and EN71-1/2/3.

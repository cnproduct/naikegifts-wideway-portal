Translating flat 2D vector logos into tactile 3D soft PVC, hard enamel cloisonné, and embroidered plush requires strict geometric tolerances. Below are our official visual identity design tokens and pre-press vector guidelines ensuring zero distortion of your corporate brand assets.

Vector DFM Guidelines for Physical Swag Production

Soft PVC Micro-Injection Rules

  • Minimum Stroke Width: 0.25mm (0.7 pt) to allow automated pneumatic needle travel.
  • Minimum Color Separation Gap: 0.35mm to prevent liquid plastisol capillary cross-contamination.
  • Maximum 3D Multi-Tier Relief: 4 distinct height planes (up to 3.5mm total relief).

Hard & Soft Enamel Metal Rules

  • Minimum Metal Wall Thickness: 0.20mm between adjoining enamel cells to retain molten mineral paste.
  • Recessed Texture Depths: 0.40mm–0.60mm for rich, reflective sandblast contrast.
  • Cutout / Pierced Hole Diameter: Minimum 1.2mm for clean punch tool clearance.
